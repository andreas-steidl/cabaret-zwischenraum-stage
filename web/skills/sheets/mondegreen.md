# Mondegreen

*German edition: Nur Bahnhof.*

*A mondegreen is a mishearing. A girl once heard the ballad line “and laid
him on the green” as “and Lady Mondegreen”, and a lady was born who never
existed. Speech recognition understands every word. Just not the one that
was said.*

## How to recognise it

- **A name that doesn't exist.** A session named after a castle keep turns
  into an English country estate. Another one into a video game princess.
  Two days, two new names out of nowhere.
- **A sentence that is correct and doesn't fit.** „Ich will die Erde küssen“
  (I want to kiss the earth) was „ich will per RDP drauf“ (I want to get on
  via RDP). Read aloud in German, *RDP* is *er-de-pe*, a breath away from
  *Erde*. Grammatically flawless, in substance a poem.
- **Technical words as everyday words.** Abbreviations and English terms
  become whichever word of the speaker's language sounds most like them.
- **Numbers and negations.** Fifteen and fifty, thirteen and thirty. A
  “not” drops out or creeps in, and the sentence means the opposite.

## How to read a dictation

1. **Hold strange names against the known ones.** The names of sessions,
   projects, machines and people are in CLAUDE.md, in memory, in the boards.
   If a word sounds like a known name, it is almost always the known name.
   Never invent a new thing just because a word is new.
2. **Read it aloud, not literally.** A strange sentence gets the question
   “How does this sound when spoken?”, not “What does this mean?”.
3. **For actions, offer your own reading.** Where a name or a number
   deletes, moves, sends or costs something: “I read: log out the account
   andreas. Correct?” Yes or no is faster than spelling.
4. **Have it spelled** only when two readings fit equally well.

## What you don't do

- Write the misheard name into files, boards or commits. A country squire
  in the board stays there forever.
- Correct the human as if it were a typo. They spoke correctly; someone
  else misheard.

## The one check

Every name and every number from a dictation that triggers an action: find
it in the project first. Whatever you can't find counts as misheard until
the human confirms it.
